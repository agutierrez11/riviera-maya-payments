Methodology and sources
The deck shape is the public cross-firm SteerCo discipline. No proprietary internal methods are cited. References fetched / verified 14 May 2026.
Methodology stack — where each slide came from
PMBOK 7th ed. (2021) [^1]
Scope & schedule baseline on slide 03 · RAID log on slide 09 · stakeholder register on slide 11.
Bain Results Delivery® [^2]
Three-question test (right things / right / value) operationalises slides 04 RAG dashboard and 12 findings.
Stage-Gate® (Cooper) [^3]
W4 / W8 / W12 gates on slide 03 are decision gates, not progress markers — approve / modify / stop at each.
Disruption portfolio phase-gates (Christensen et al.) [^4]
HBR (2015) phase-gate analog — early-redirect-not-late-rescue logic encoded in W4 scope confirmation.
Execution discipline (Bossidy & Charan, 2002) [^5]
Named-owner accountability on every Gantt row (slide 03) and dependency row (slide 10) — "named owners or no decision."
McKinsey transformation research [^6]
Approximately 70% of large transformations fail to meet objectives; governance discipline is consistently cited among top mitigations. This deck's slide-04→slide-13 flow is the structural answer.
APM BoK 7th ed. (2019) [^7]
UK Association for Project Management — most explicit codification of the 4-column RAID structure used on slide 09.
RACI matrix [^8]
Responsibility-assignment vocabulary (Responsible / Accountable / Consulted / Informed) used in named-owner columns.
