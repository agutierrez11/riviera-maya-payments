# Write a Kickoff and SteerCo Deck like a Tier-1 Strategy Project Manager

> Day 1 the client sees 12 weeks; every SteerCo after is green, yellow, or red.

![kickoff-steerco-deck methodology illustration](https://cdn1.genspark.ai/user-upload-image/slide_agent/v2-catalog-hero/085-bain-kickoff-steerco-status.png)

## Why this skill works

- **By 09:30 on Day 1 the SteerCo knows the 12-week path.** A single Gantt with workstreams + owners + decision gates lets the room argue about scope, not chronology.
- **Each subsequent SteerCo is 15 minutes, not an hour.** RAG dashboard at slide 04 means status reads in 30 seconds; red items get the next 14 minutes, not the green ones.
- **You bring the scope + the workstreams + the sponsor.** Tell the agent the engagement, the four (rarely five) workstreams, and the SteerCo cadence; it returns the deck that runs all three update flavors with cover-sheet swap only.

## Methodology cheat-sheet

**Workplan × RAG × Decision Gate** — three habits that turn a status meeting into a steering meeting.

1. **PMBOK / PMI**[^1] — the PMI Project Management Body of Knowledge is the cross-industry reference for project management. Concepts the deck operationalizes: scope baseline, schedule baseline, RAID log (Risks, Assumptions, Issues, Dependencies), stakeholder register, decision authority. The Gantt on slide 03 and the RAID-style risks on slide 09 follow PMBOK vocabulary.

2. **Top-tier consulting results-delivery approach**[^2] — the canonical tier-1 strategy change-management discipline emphasizes the three-question test: are we doing the right things, are we doing them right, are we delivering the value? The deck's slide 04 (RAG) and slide 12 (Findings + Value Tracking) operationalize the third question.

3. **The SteerCo discipline**[^3] — codified across MBB and large management consulting houses. The discipline has three rules: (a) green items get zero air time, (b) every red item is escalated with a decision request, (c) every SteerCo ends with a decisions log. The deck's slide 04 → slide 13 flow is the literal implementation.

4. **Decision gates**[^4] — phase-gate / stage-gate review points (Cooper's Stage-Gate® being the canonical commercial version). The deck's week 4 / 8 / 12 gates allow the SteerCo to redirect or stop the engagement at known checkpoints, rather than discovering misalignment at week 11.

5. **The one habit that makes SteerCo time productive** — write the decisions on slide 13 BEFORE the SteerCo, with the recommended choice pre-filled. The SteerCo's job is to approve, modify, or defer; not to invent decisions in the room.

## Before / After

### A bi-weekly SteerCo, week 6, post-merger integration engagement

**Typical PPT template**

> **Project Update — Week 6**
>
> - Activities completed this period
> - Progress on each workstream
> - Upcoming activities
> - Risks and issues
> - Q&A

**This skill's rewrite (slide 04, RAG Dashboard)**

> **Status as of 14 May 2026 (Week 6 of 12).**
>
> | Workstream | Status | Reason | Goes to slide |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | WS1 — Day-1 Org Design | 🟢 GREEN | On track; 90% of leadership backfills closed | 05 |
> | WS2 — Systems Integration | 🟡 AMBER | 1 critical app integration slipped 2 weeks (vendor SLA) | 06 + 13 (Decision #1) |
> | WS3 — Customer Retention | 🔴 RED | NA top-20 accounts NPS dropped 8 pts; need pricing exception authority | 07 + 13 (Decision #2) |
> | WS4 — Synergy Tracking | 🟢 GREEN | $42M run-rate vs $40M plan | 08 |
>
> Two of four green. Today's SteerCo: spend 90% of time on WS3 and WS2.

### Closing slide of the same SteerCo

**Typical PPT template**

> **Next Steps**
>
> - Continue execution
> - Follow up on action items
> - Monitor risks
> - Reconvene in two weeks

**This skill's rewrite (slide 13, Decisions Required Today)**

> **Two decisions required from this SteerCo:**
>
> 1. **WS2 critical app delay — approve workaround OR re-baseline week-8 gate by 2 weeks.** Recommended: **approve workaround** (parallel manual reconciliation through week 10). Cost: $0.4M temporary FTE. **Owner if approved: CIO + WS2 lead.** **[ ] Approve / [ ] Modify / [ ] Defer**
>
> 2. **WS3 retention — grant pricing-exception authority of up to 12% discount on NA top-20 for Q2.** Recommended: **approve**, with sponsor weekly check-in. Cost: estimated $2.8M revenue exposure, against $14M churn-risk if not granted. **Owner if approved: CRO + Sponsor.** **[ ] Approve / [ ] Modify / [ ] Defer**
>
> Decisions logged to project register; next SteerCo: 28 May 2026.

## What this skill produces

A 14-slide, 1920×1080 HTML deck designed for a 60-90 minute Day-1 Kickoff, a 30-minute bi-weekly SteerCo, or a 45-minute Monthly Progress to a sponsor / Audit Committee — the same body slides, three different cover/header variants. The deck enforces three non-negotiable disciplines drawn from PMI PMBOK[^1], the canonical tier-1 strategy results-delivery approach[^2], and the cross-firm SteerCo tradition[^3][^4]:

1. **12-Week Gantt with Owners on slide 03** — four (rarely five) workstreams, each with a named lead on both consultant side and client side, decision-gate milestones at week 4 / 8 / 12, dependencies explicit. The Gantt is the deck's single most-quoted slide; clients screenshot this one.
2. **RAG Dashboard on slide 04** — every workstream + every milestone shows Red / Amber / Green with a one-line reason. Red items roll up to "Decisions Required Today" at slide 13. **Green items get no air time**; the SteerCo discipline is to spend zero seconds on green.
3. **Decisions Required Today on slide 13** — 1-3 binary asks of the SteerCo, each with the recommended decision pre-filled. Without this slide the SteerCo is a status update, not a steering meeting.
