# Pitch Event Sponsorship like a Top Sports & Entertainment Agency SVP

> Sell what the logo will *do*, not where it will go — CFO-signable, not CPM-priced.

![event-sponsorship-pitch-deck methodology illustration](https://cdn1.genspark.ai/user-upload-image/slide_agent/v2-catalog-hero/129-wasserman-sponsorship-pitch.png)

## Why this skill works

- **The CMO leans forward at slide 2.** The deck opens on the brand's growth problem — verbatim, attributed — before naming a single property asset.
- **The CFO signs by slide 13.** Pricing is anchored on the exclusivity grant and a comparable case, with a six-lever financial-contribution model, not impressions.
- **You bring 1 property + 1 brand + 1 growth problem.** Tell it the property, the target brand, and the CMO/CEO-stated growth problem; it returns a 14-slide pitch with named activations and a defensible measurement plan.

## Methodology cheat-sheet

**Six-Lever Contribution × Activation Map × Exclusivity-Priced** — a sponsorship-pitch shape that frames the deal as financial contribution to the brand's growth problem, not as impression-value arbitrage[^1].

1. **Sell the brand's problem first**[^1] — Diorio's Forbes thesis: the deal exists to solve a stated growth problem, not to expose a logo. Slide 2 is the brand's CMO/CEO-stated problem, quoted.
2. **Activation > inventory**[^2][^3] — only 57% of brands actively measure total sponsorship exposure value; the difference is named activations vs static logo placement. Logos in stadium are inventory; named activations convert impressions to contribution.
3. **Six contribution levers, not impression counts**[^1][^2] — brand equity / demand gen / category access / talent / IP / employee engagement. The deck commits to 3 of 6 and defends each.
4. **Anchor on a comparable case in the brand's category**[^3] — the published 2024 women's-sports +22% YoY sponsorship-momentum data supplies the kind of numeric anchor a senior brand expects on slide 3.
5. **Attribute properly**[^6] — Relo Metrics' modern attribution stack: aided awareness, consideration, category-share lift, social SOV. Every outcome on the measurement plan names its instrument.
6. **Category exclusivity is the price anchor** — pricing is set against the exclusivity grant and a comparable-case benchmark, not against raw CPMs. IEG/Escalent supplies the canon for the valuation method[^5].

## What this skill produces

A 14-slide, 1920×1080 reference pitch deck for a 2-5 year category-exclusive partnership pitch. The visual mode is editorial sports — slate `#1A1F2B`, signal red `#D7263D`, electric blue `#2B6BFF` on white, with deep green `#0E5C3F` as the financial-pitch secondary. Condensed display sans for property/brand names, grotesk body, tabular numerals everywhere, full-bleed earned-and-credited action photography on hero slides. No clip-art, no generic stadium stock.

The deck enforces three non-negotiable disciplines: (i) slide 2 is the brand's problem in the brand's words — quoted, attributed (CMO/CEO earnings call, RFP, public commentary), dated; (ii) every named asset is rendered as *Outcome → Property mechanism → Measurement instrument* and plotted on the 4-quadrant Activation Map (Awareness × Consideration × Category-share × LTV); (iii) the deck commits to at least 3 of Diorio's six financial-contribution levers — never "all six" (which is overpromising) and never "impressions and awareness" (which is CPM-pricing in disguise).
