# Write a New Product GTM Deck like a Tier-1 Strategy GTM Strategist

> Segmentation × Targeting × Positioning — pick one beach-head, defer everyone else.

![new-product-gtm-deck methodology illustration](https://cdn1.genspark.ai/user-upload-image/slide_agent/v2-catalog-hero/066-a16z-gtm-stp.png)

## Why this skill works

- **The board stops asking "who is this for, exactly?".** A scored segmentation matrix forces the team to name the beach-head out loud, with the math defended on a single slide.
- **Sales stops chasing every inbound that looks vaguely like ICP.** Picking one segment and explicitly deferring the others is the discipline that makes the first 100 customers concentrated enough to learn from.
- **You bring a product and 3-5 candidate buyer segments.** Tell the agent the product, the candidate segments, and the founder's gut beach-head; it returns a board-ready GTM plan.

## Methodology cheat-sheet

**STP × Beach-head × Concentration** — a GTM deck shape that forces the team to commit to a single beach-head and defer the rest, with the scoring math defended explicitly.

1. **Kotler's STP framework**[^1] — Philip Kotler's foundational marketing canon: Segmentation (identify distinct buyer groups), Targeting (pick which to serve), Positioning (define how to be seen in their mind). The deck's three required slides are direct STP operationalisation.

2. **Moore's *Crossing the Chasm***[^2] — Geoffrey Moore's beach-head strategy: pre-chasm sales cycle requires *concentration* on a single segment until the team wins it, then *adjacency expansion* to the next segment using the first segment's references. The deck enforces single-beach-head selection.

3. **Tier-1 strategy and applied-AI VC GTM playbooks**[^3] — published consulting and VC playbooks emphasise scoring segments on TAM × Accessibility × Willingness-to-pay, with explicit deferral of the unselected. The slide-06 matrix follows the canonical "GTM Diamond" three-axis convention.

4. **Concentration-curve evidence for early-stage startups**[^4] — First Round Capital's *State of Startups* and Lenny Rachitsky's GTM data: startups whose first 50 customers concentrate in one ICP segment grow 2.3× faster than those whose first 50 customers spread across multiple segments. The single-beach-head rule is empirically grounded.

5. **Pricing-as-positioning research**[^5] — March / Sutton on pricing as a positioning signal. Pricing tiers and discount discipline communicate buyer-tier targeting more than positioning statements do. Slide 16 is therefore required.

## Before / After

### Slide 06 — Segmentation

**Typical PPT template**

> **Target Markets**
>
> - Enterprise (Fortune 500)
> - Mid-market (500-5000 employees)
> - SMB (50-500)
> - Public sector
> - Healthcare
> - Financial services

**This skill's rewrite**

> **Segmentation matrix — scored on 3 axes (0-10)**
>
> | Segment | TAM ($) | Access (0-10) | WtP (0-10) | Composite |
> |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
> | F500 — Banking | $4.2B | 3 | 9 | 5.9 |
> | F500 — Healthcare | $3.8B | 4 | 7 | 5.4 |
> | Mid-market — Tech | $2.4B | 8 | 7 | **7.3** |
> | Mid-market — Retail | $1.8B | 6 | 5 | 5.0 |
> | SMB — Tech | $0.9B | 9 | 4 | 5.1 |
>
> *Source*: TAM from <provider>; Access and WtP from 84 customer interviews.

### Slide 09 — Targeting

**Typical PPT template**

> **Our Approach**
>
> - We will target multiple segments
> - Phase 1 will focus on enterprise
> - Phase 2 will expand to mid-market
> - Phase 3 will include SMB

**This skill's rewrite**

> **Beach-head: Mid-market Tech (250-2500 employees) — and nothing else for 12 months.**
>
> *Why*: highest composite score; channel-fit (LinkedIn ABM + founder-led
> sales) is already validated; WtP signal from 3 pilot customers at
> $84-110k ACV.
>
> *Phase 2 trigger (Q5-Q6)*: 100 paying customers in beach-head + GRR
> ≥ 92% + NPS ≥ 45. Then enable Mid-market Retail.
>
> *Until then*: F500 inbound is qualified out. SMB inbound is
> qualified out. The team's energy is one segment.

## What this skill produces

A 22-slide, 1920×1080 deck for new-product GTM planning, post-PMF expansion planning, the GTM sub-section of a Series B+ board deck, or a market-entry section of a strategy off-site. The visual mode is consulting-clean: white background, navy + accent, the STP matrix as the visual anchor, scenario tables for sequencing.

The deck enforces three non-negotiable disciplines drawn from Kotler's Segmentation × Targeting × Positioning canon[^1], Geoffrey Moore's *Crossing the Chasm* beach-head strategy[^2], and the published GTM playbooks of tier-1 strategy consultancies and tier-1 applied-AI VCs[^3]:

1. **Segmentation matrix with TAM × Accessibility × Willingness-to-pay scoring** — each candidate segment is scored on three axes; "we will target all segments" is rewritten.
2. **Targeting slide that picks ONE beach-head** — explicitly defers the other segments to phase 2 / phase 3, with the criteria for moving from phase 1 to phase 2.
3. **Positioning Statement in canonical form** — `For [target] who [need], [product] is [category] that [unique benefit], unlike [alternative]`. Multi-clause hedge positioning is rewritten.
