20 · RISKS · NAMED EXPERIMENTS · KILL CRITERIA
Three failure modes. Each has an experiment that disproves it.
R1
FAILURE MODE
Forecast accuracy decays at scale.
14 cohort customers showed 9.4% median error, but n is small. At 100 customers, mid-quarter rep behaviour drift may degrade accuracy beyond 18% in some segments.
EXPERIMENT (4 WEEKS)
Blind backtest on 20 prospect pipelines.
Owner: VP Eng + RevOps lead. Run Predict against 4 trailing quarters of prospect data; compare to their actuals; produce dispersion histogram.
KILL CRITERION
If > 30% of cases exceed 18% error.
→ pause guarantee marketing; engineering recalibrates before next 20 customers signed.
R2
FAILURE MODE
Clari counter-launches a mid-market SKU.
Clari has the brand and the cash to ship a mid-market-priced variant within 6 months and bundle it into existing seats. Our differentiation collapses on price.
EXPERIMENT (6 WEEKS)
10 paid head-to-head bake-offs.
Owner: VP Sales. Offer 10 named prospects a $5K credit to run Predict alongside their existing Clari instance for 30 days. Measure delta in board-ready quality.
KILL CRITERION
If < 5 of 10 prefer Predict.
→ rewrite slide 12 alternative; pivot positioning to a workflow Clari does not occupy (CFO-side audit).
R3
FAILURE MODE
Sales cycle is longer than 84 days.
CFO involvement may stretch procurement timelines. If median cycle exceeds 120 days, the cash plan and headcount triggers slip by a quarter.
EXPERIMENT (2 WEEKS)
CFO-first sales sprint.
Owner: Founder + Head of Finance. Lead 6 named conversations with the CFO before the CRO. Compare cycle length vs. the RevOps-first control group.
KILL CRITERION
If CFO-first delta ≥ +21 days.
→ retain RevOps-first sequencing; the CFO becomes a stage-3 stakeholder, not a stage-1 buyer.
