WHAT WE HEARD
01
Forecast accuracy is a board-meeting problem, not a CRO problem. 41 of 84 leaders said the most painful conversation of their quarter is being asked "are you going to hit?" with no defensible answer.
02
Clari is the named alternative — but only at scale. 22 of 84 evaluated Clari; 17 walked away citing "too rep-inspection-heavy for our 18-person sales org."
03
The spreadsheet is the incumbent. 64 of 84 still produce the board forecast in Google Sheets or Excel, manually pulling from Salesforce reports.
04
Buying authority sits with the CRO, not the RevOps lead. RevOps owns the evaluation; the CRO signs the contract. Our champion programme must reflect both roles.
05
12-week sales cycle is realistic. Median time from first-call to signature across 11 closed-won design partners was 84 days.
THE SURPRISE
The CFO showed up in 29 of 84 conversations — unprompted. Forecast accuracy is increasingly a finance-led purchase in mid-market. We have re-shaped messaging for slide 13 accordingly.
