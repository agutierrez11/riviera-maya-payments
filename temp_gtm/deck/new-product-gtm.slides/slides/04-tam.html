04 · TOTAL ADDRESSABLE MARKET
Triangulated to a single number we will defend.
Three independent methods. We reconcile to the conservative figure and explain the deltas.
METHOD 01 · TOP-DOWN
Global RevOps software spend × wedge
Gartner places sales-tech spend at $32.4B for 2025. Forecasting + revenue-intelligence wedge ≈ 11% of that surface (Forrester WAVE 2025). Apply wedge → $3.56B. Filter for mid-market 250–2,500 FTE buyers (38% of the wedge per Gartner segment cut).
RESULT
$1.35B
METHOD 02 · BOTTOM-UP
Accounts × ACV in beach-head & adj.
Pitchbook + Crunchbase: 34,200 US/EMEA companies in the 250–2,500 FTE band running CRM (Salesforce or HubSpot Enterprise). Realistic SAM = 22,800 after filtering for ≥$50M revenue. Anchor ACV $58K (median of 14 design-partner contracts).
RESULT
$1.32B
METHOD 03 · ANALOG
Clari + Gong forecasting revenue × share
Clari ARR ≈ $250M (Forbes 2024 estimate), Gong forecast wedge ≈ $180M (S-1 read), Aviso $40M, BoostUp $25M. Total $495M at ~35% mid-market mix → $173M realised. Implied TAM if penetration reaches 10% = $1.73B.
RESULT
$1.73B
RECONCILED TAM (CONSERVATIVE)
$1.32B.
