03 · PRODUCT SUMMARY
Forecastly Predict in two sentences.

Predict ingests opportunity-level signal from Salesforce, Outreach, Gong, and the GL ledger and produces a deterministic 90-day pipeline forecast that the CRO can defend in a board meeting — accurate to within ±12% of actuals.

It replaces the spreadsheet-and-Slack ritual most mid-market RevOps teams run on the last Friday of every month with a single signed forecast, an audit trail, and a confidence band tied to deal-level evidence.
